About SignBridge AI

Our goal is to reduce communication barriers in classrooms. This prototype demonstrates speech recognition, keyword matching, webcam hand-landmark tracking and text-to-speech. Full ISL vocabulary recognition and a true 3D signing avatar are future development features.
